Working capital

We carry the stock

This releases cash in their business. Reach makes the profit over time. They may pay a little more. They do not tie cash up in a back room.

The offer

Reach holds the stock. Their cash stays in the business. The product is there when the service needs it.

The long game

Once the stock and the cash sit with Reach, a kitchen does not go back to filling a room. That is the profit, earned over time. It is not a win in week one.

The limit

Hold only what turns. The premium has to cover the cost of carrying it. Do not fund a line that has not earned its place.
